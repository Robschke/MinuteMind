## What's new

- **Home and a sidebar per project.** Home shows every project as a tile. Inside a project the sidebar shows its sections (Overview, Workspace, Jobs), its open Claude sessions and its files. The back and forward buttons of your mouse step through where you have been.
- **Search everything with Cmd+K.** Find pages, settings, projects, Claude sessions and menu commands, and meetings by their title, by what was said in the transcript or written in the summary, plus your knowledge files. Works in the terminal and the editor too.
- **Switch projects with Option+Tab.** Hold Option and press Tab, like Cmd+Tab. The switcher shows your starred projects and every project with Claude running, each with its name. Shift+Tab stays free for Claude Code's modes.
- **Agents in the project sidebar.** Each open Claude session is listed with its status (working, waiting for you, idle). A click takes you straight to it.
- **Projects are their creature.** Projects no longer have a colour of their own; their creature identifies them everywhere. Right click a project and choose Edit Project... to change its name, its companion and the creature's colour, with any colour from the system colour panel. Existing colours carry over.
- **More room.** Pages reach up beside the window buttons, and the top of the sidebar is one colour.
