## What's new

- **Switch projects with Ctrl+Tab.** The project switcher moved from Option+Tab to Ctrl+Tab, because launchers such as Raycast take Option+Tab for themselves. Hold Control and press Tab, like Cmd+Tab. Shift+Tab stays free for Claude Code's modes.
- **Creatures show what Claude is doing.** On Home and in a project's Agents list, the project's creature types while a Claude session works, waves while one waits for you, holds its thumb up with a green check once a turn has finished, and sleeps while its sessions are idle. Home tiles no longer need a separate status line.
- **Starred projects** show a yellow star after their name in the sidebar.

## Fixes

- The Claude tabs and the buttons beside them show again at the top of a project's Workspace in a normal window with the sidebar shown.
