## What's new

- **Tasks and Apple Reminders stay in sync.** Tick a task off in Reminders and it is done in MinuteMind too, and the other way round. Titles and due dates changed in Reminders come back, a reminder deleted there closes its task, and reminders you add to the "MinuteMind Tasks" list become tasks. New in Settings, Automation, Reminders Sync: "Sync all open tasks to Apple Reminders" keeps every open task from every project in that list; a task's bell takes it out. With the switch off, only tasks whose bell you turn on are synced, as before.
- **The Reminders list reaches your iPhone.** A new "MinuteMind Tasks" list is created in your iCloud account instead of only on this Mac.

## Fixes

- **Updating from the app works again.** Update no longer keeps saying "Updating…" without anything happening: MinuteMind now quits, installs the new version and opens again. If an update cannot finish, the dialog says why.
- The update command in Terminal explains what to do when MinuteMind does not quit, for example when macOS asks whether Terminal may control MinuteMind.
