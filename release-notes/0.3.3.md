## What's new

- **Agent cockpit.** A new Cockpit entry in the sidebar lists every project with an open Claude session, each session shown by its project's creature. Pick a project to work in its Claude tabs right there, without leaving the cockpit. Creatures jump while a session waits for you. Cmd+N starts a new Claude session, Cmd+T starts one in any project.
- **Themes by prompt.** Ask Claude in a workspace session for a theme and it designs one for you: colours, font, corners, spacing and terminal font. The whole app follows the theme, terminals and the editor included. Theme files show in Settings, Appearance next to the built-in themes.
- **Your own avatars, in any style.** Ask Claude to design a companion avatar, pixel art or a drawing. Every avatar, built-in or your own, can be deleted with a right click in the picker, and Settings, Appearance can restore the built-in ones. The companion's size now has a slider.
- **Sort the sidebar by recent use.** Settings, Appearance, Sidebar can order projects by their most recent Claude sessions, starred projects first.
- **Better transcripts of calls.** Your microphone and the call audio are now lined up in time, so turns appear in the right order. When the microphone picks up the call from your speakers, those doubled words are dropped. Speakers change at sentence ends instead of mid-sentence.
- **Dictation keeps your first words.** The microphone now opens as soon as you press the key, so the start of a sentence is no longer cut off.
